§ The Story
V-003

Soft, as a deliberate
position.

Madagascan vanilla pods take two years to cure. The Sambava cooperative hand-pollinates 400,000 flowers each season — one of the most labour-intensive agricultural acts still performed. We use whole-pod extract, not synthetic vanillin.

Crème is our most conservative formulation — low pH, high shea, minimal abrasion. It is the bar we give to friends with dry winter skin, and to those visiting our atelier who do not yet know what to make of us. A diplomatic first taste.

Reads as warm milk before it boils. Sugar arrives last — fine, crystalline, never candy-sweet. The vanilla is pod-first: floral, almost leathery at the finish.

ii.

Silken.

The highest shea load of the three. Lather is soft, cloud-like, more cashmere than canvas. Moves across the skin with almost no mechanical resistance. Gentle by design.

iii.

Soft.

Skin reads as moisturized without residue. Sillage of warm pastry holds for thirty minutes — understated, pairs with linen, not perfume. Safe for face, neck, décolletage.

§ Suggested Ritual

Anytime. Any skin.
No apology.

Velvet Crème is our all-hours bar. Gentle enough for the face, rich enough for full-body use. Suitable for sensitive skin, children older than three, and anyone recovering from over-exfoliation.

Taste first — a grain of the bar reads as warm cream. Then lather in tepid water, apply with an open palm rather than a washcloth. Pat dry; do not rub. The sillage is subtle, domestic, reassuring — pairs with morning coffee or a freshly made bed.
