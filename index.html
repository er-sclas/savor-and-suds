§ 01
The Manifesto

The Dual-Nature Paradox.

A Brief Thesis

Why must hygiene taste of punishment?

For two centuries, cleanliness has been chemically hostile — sodium lauryl sulfates, petrochemical fragrances, dyes synthesized in industrial labs. The modern bar of soap is an object we are taught to fear tasting.

Savor & Suds rejects that inheritance. We asked a simple, deliberate question: what if the bar in your shower was as safe as the confection on your dessert plate?

Principle I i.

Edible ≠ Inferior.

Food-grade saponins, derived from Quillaja bark and yucca root, cleanse at 30% the efficacy of commercial lye soaps — more than enough for daily use, while remaining biologically harmless when tasted.

Principle II ii.

Flavor is honesty.

Our bars disclose their chemistry by taste. A Mango Nectar bar reads as Alphonso puree. A Noir Cacao bar, as 70% dark. If you can taste it, you know what it is.

Principle III iii.

Ritual over routine.

We are not selling a utility. We are selling a moment — a five-minute suspension where washing becomes tasting, and the body is treated as a palate, not a problem.

§ 05
Frequently Asked

Reasonable doubts.

Is it actually safe to eat? +

Yes. Every ingredient is certified food-grade and the bar passes Indian FSSAI food safety thresholds. We do not, however, recommend it as a substitute for actual dessert — taste it, don't dine on it.

Does it actually clean? +

To roughly 30% of standard lye-based soap efficacy — which is more than adequate for daily hygiene of healthy skin. If you are working in a surgical theater, please use surgical soap.

Why the price? +

Food-grade surfactants are an order of magnitude more expensive than industrial ones. We cure for forty days. Each bar is hand-pressed. The price is the cost of the object, not a markup on its meaning.

How long does a bar last? +

Approximately six weeks of daily use. Store dry, away from direct sunlight, on a draining dish. Never submerge.

Do you ship internationally? +

Yes — worldwide, carbon-neutral. Free above ₹14,999. Bars are cold-shipped during summer months to preserve flavor stability.
